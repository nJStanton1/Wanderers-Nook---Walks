## Overview

A wonderful little town reachable from Victoria Train Station in 25 minutes. There are plenty of cafes and pubs in the town that provide a great place to recharge after a good walk. A short walk from the station and you'll find yourself surrounded by greenery and exploring the many valley streams that feed into Hollingworth Lake. 

### Best Views:

* **Hollingworth Lake:** Up close, you'll see the local water sports clubs using the lake. From afar, it gives a lovely tranquil spot to see how far you've walked.
* **Rochdale Canal:** Originally starting in Manchester, this canal stretches right through Littleborough and deep into the countryside. A nice place for an easy gander.
* **Blackstone Edge:** A high trig point for the peakbaggers among you that marks the boundary between Lancashire and Yorkshire. Provides a spectacular view of the moors and Rochdale.
* **Whitelin Owls:** A waterfall nestled in forest provides a place of peaceful reflection.

### Amenities:

* **Parking:** Parking is available in the town, but is largely pay and display.
* **Toilets:** Many cafes/pubs have easy access toilets. There are public toilets by tourist areas. Walks into the pennines will take you away from anywhere with toilets.
* **Eating:** There are plenty of cafes and pubs you can visit within the town, and most tourist spots like the lake have their own cafe.
* **Bridleways:** There are many bridleways through this area of the pennines. This makes it a greta spot for horse riding, cycling and walking.
