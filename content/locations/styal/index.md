## Highlights:

* **Quarry Bank:** An industrial age mill built by Samuel Greg. It sits in a valley of woodlands and gardens, providing a juxtaposition of landscapes. As a National Trust site, there is plenty of local history available, as well as a café and well signposted routes.
* **Airport Viewing:** Further out from Styal is Manchester Airport. Accessible from Styal is a viewing platform from which you can view a full runway.
* **River Bollin:** The river Bollin runs through the area. It provides several good walking routes and several beautiful viewing spots. It also provided much of the power for the machines of the industrial age mill.

## Amenities:

* **Parking:** Parking is available in the town, but is limited and largely pay and display.
* **Toilets:** The National Trust site has freely accessible toilets, and the local village has a pub with toilets. However, there aren't any public toilets in the town itself, and the station has no facilities.
* **Pubs:** The Ship Inn is located in the village and is a lovely place to finish any walks.
* **Café:** There is a great National Trust café located next to Quarry Bank Mill which provides hot beverages and jacket potatoes to fill up before or after a walk.
